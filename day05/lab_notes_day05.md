Day 5. Built an LP portfolio summary generator that takes structured portfolio data aggregated from Days 1 through 4 and produces a professional quarterly update in the register of a senior credit professional. The prompt explicitly instructed the model not to soften covenant breaches and to write for a sophisticated institutional audience — a pension fund or family office that expects candour.

The output was the strongest the lab has produced. The active positions section named all four Project Apex covenant breaches explicitly, quantified the EBITDA deterioration at 18%, flagged the add-back ratio and noted that the origination triage flags had proven material. That last observation — that the early warning system worked and the risk was visible at underwriting — emerged from the model synthesising Days 1 and 4 outputs without being explicitly instructed to make the connection.

The risk commentary named concentration risk directly, treated the borrower's recovery thesis with stated scepticism and confirmed external counsel engagement. The declined deals section was one sentence because mandate discipline requires no elaboration.

The key prompt engineering lesson from this day: the instruction "do not soften them" produced a materially different output than a neutral instruction would have. Role framing and explicit behavioural constraints shape not just the format but the analytical courage of the output. An LP update written by a model told to be candid reads differently from one told to summarise. Same data, different instruction, different document.
