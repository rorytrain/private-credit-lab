CREDIT AGREEMENT — PROJECT APEX
Unitranche Term Loan Facility
Date: June 2026

PARTIES
Borrower: Project Apex Holdings Limited
Lender: Silverstone Credit Partners LP
Facility Amount: $65,000,000

FINANCIAL COVENANTS

1. LEVERAGE COVENANT
The Borrower shall not permit the Total Net Leverage Ratio to exceed:
- 4.50x for the period ending September 2026
- 4.25x for the period ending December 2026
- 4.00x for any period thereafter
Tested quarterly on a Last Twelve Months basis.

2. MINIMUM LIQUIDITY
The Borrower shall maintain minimum unrestricted cash and available
revolving credit of no less than $3,000,000 at all times.
Tested monthly.

3. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE RESTRICTION
Annual capital expenditure shall not exceed $4,000,000 in any
financial year without prior written lender consent.

4. RESTRICTED PAYMENTS TEST
No dividend, distribution or shareholder payment shall be made unless:
(a) No Event of Default is continuing or would result therefrom
(b) Total Net Leverage Ratio is below 3.50x on a pro forma basis
(c) Minimum liquidity of $5,000,000 is maintained post-payment

REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
Monthly management accounts: within 30 days of month end
Quarterly compliance certificate: within 45 days of quarter end
Annual audited accounts: within 120 days of financial year end

EVENTS OF DEFAULT
Standard events of default apply including payment default,
covenant breach, insolvency and material adverse change.
Cure period: 30 days for financial covenant breaches.
