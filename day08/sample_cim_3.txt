CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION MEMORANDUM - TEASER
Project Meridian | June 2026

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Meridian is an independent petrol station and convenience retail network
operating across 187 sites in the UK Midlands and North West. The business has
demonstrated stable footfall supported by long-term fuel supply agreements with
two major oil majors. The sponsor, Bridgepoint, acquired the business in 2019.

2. FINANCIAL OVERVIEW

Management reports the following for the Last Twelve Months (LTM) ending March 2026:

Total Revenue: $312.0 million
Adjusted EBITDA: $18.4 million (inclusive of $4.1 million in management add-backs)
Unadjusted EBITDA: $14.3 million
Gross Margin: 6%
Implied Leverage (Facility / Adjusted EBITDA): 5.8x

3. TRANSACTION OVERVIEW

Bridgepoint is seeking a $106.7 million Senior Secured Term Loan B to fund a
bolt-on acquisition of a 34-site regional competitor and refinance existing
senior debt.
Target close: Q4 2026.
Indicative leverage tolerance: 6.0x closing EBITDA.
